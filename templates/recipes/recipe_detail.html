{% comment %}Preparing and cooking, never both at once — and that is what ended the duplication. The ingredients used to be on the page twice: once as the list down the left, and again inside the diagram beside it, which is where the diagram *puts* them. Two answers to "what goes in this" on one screen, and the scaler rewriting both, is one answer too many. So they are split by the question being asked. Preparing is what to buy and get out: the list, scaled, measured against the cupboard. Cooking is the method, which already carries each line beside the step that consumes it. Both are rendered and one is hidden, rather than being two pages: switching is something somebody does with wet hands halfway through, and a round trip there loses the servings they had scaled to.{% endcomment %}
