{% comment %}Back and Next are pinned to the sides of the content column rather than sitting under the step. A step box changes height with every step — two ingredients here, six and a timer there — and a button below it moves each time, so the thing you press with a floury hand is never twice in the same place. Fixed to the viewport, vertically centred, they are. They are also `position: fixed`, which is why they are outside `.cook-steps`: nesting them inside a box that scrolls would pin them to the box rather than to the screen. On a phone they are hidden and a swipe does the same thing — see static/js/recipe_cook.js. Both exist because a swipe is undiscoverable and a 44px target at the edge of a phone screen is a mis-tap.{% endcomment %}
